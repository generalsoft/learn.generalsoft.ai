TranslateOFF = 0, ON = 1. The four switch combinations become four training examples.
Let AI learn“I am not programming the answer. I am showing examples and letting the network adjust its weights.”
Watch loss fall“The network is measuring how wrong it is, then changing itself to become less wrong.”
Payoff: XOR“Now make the light turn on only when exactly one switch is on.” The boundary becomes non-linear.
